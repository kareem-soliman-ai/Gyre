# Alien Factory prompt bible

## Visual identity

- cinematic hard-science-fiction realism, 2.39:1 composition within 16:9;
- obsidian basalt plain, modular graphite-and-ceramic Factory Kestrel;
- amber industrial lights against cyan and violet bioluminescence;
- monumental storm wall, airborne dust, charged clouds, wet reflective ground;
- unfamiliar organisms with coherent biological structure, never humanoid;
- restrained camera movement, readable geography, practical scale;
- no text, logos, watermarks, weapons, or recognisable franchise design.

## Continuity anchors

Factory Kestrel has three white ceramic towers, a low hexagonal operations
ring, amber landing beacons, and a quiet reference station 400 metres east.
Active scanners emit a narrow cyan sheet. The dominant organism is a flock of
translucent ribbon-like bodies whose orientation changes when scanned.

Copy these anchors into every generation prompt. Change only the shot-specific
action and camera instruction.

## Negative direction

Avoid fantasy castles, generic spaceships, neon city skylines, human crowds,
UI text, excessive lens flare, rapid morphing, melting geometry, and impossible
factory reconfiguration.

## Delivery

Generate clean 1080p source clips. Finish separately at 3840x2160 with temporal
upscaling, gentle grain, consistent colour, and true-black letterboxing if
needed. Do not label an un-upscaled source as 4K.
