# Alien Factory promotional shot list

Each film is a self-contained 8-15 second hook with no explanatory text baked
into the image. Add titles in the edit.

| ID | Title | Visual beat | Camera and motion | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AF-01 | Arrival | Factory Kestrel descends as the hurricane fills the horizon | slow aerial push, dust rolling beneath modules | “Build the loop before the storm arrives.” |
| AF-02 | The Scan Looks Back | cyan scan crosses a ribbon flock; the flock turns toward the station | locked geography, slow orbit, organism response after scan | “What if measuring the world changes it?” |
| AF-03 | Quiet Reference | active station glows while a distant dark station watches undisturbed | lateral dolly revealing both sites | “Every measurement needs a counterfactual.” |
| AF-04 | Storm Lock | shutters close, towers brace, organisms cluster around warm exhaust | ground-level push toward operations ring | “The explanation is not the outcome.” |
| AF-05 | Name the Unknown | three phenomena cross frame with radically different movement | deliberate tracking shot, no labels in render | “You may name them. The world decides what they are.” |
| AF-06 | Control Surface | human silhouette and autonomous systems choose probes and reversible controls | over-shoulder without readable UI | “Who should decide when neither side knows enough?” |

Initial Higgsfield production targets AF-01, AF-02, and AF-04. They establish
place, observer effect, and consequence.
