# Reviewer 2 design audit

## Recommendation

Proceed as a corrected structural qualification.

## Major points

1. **Self-labelled dimensions can be gamed.** Duplicate dimensions are
   penalized, briefs fully specify non-hidden facts, and runtime probes test
   applied control rather than label presence alone.
2. **EXP-03 informed the repair.** EXP-04 uses fresh tasks and is explicitly a
   new qualification, not a re-score of old outputs.
3. **The intervention is still verbose.** Template and full-skill arms share
   assets; completion-token and cost-per-success gates remain binding.
4. **The construct remains structural.** A pass would qualify the public
   prompting contract for developer trials, not prove real joint capability or
   validate private sensors.
5. **Power remains limited.** Practical gates and model-level non-regression
   carry the decision; p-values are descriptive.
